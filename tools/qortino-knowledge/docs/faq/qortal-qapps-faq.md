## Q: How do I open Q-Mail?

A: Open Q-Mail from the Apps section in Qortal Hub or Qortal GO, or type qortal://APP/Q-Mail in the search bar. Q-Mail asks Hub to sign you in; accept it, because mail is read and sent under your registered Qortal name.

## Q: How do I send a Q-Mail?

A: 1. Open Q-Mail and press Compose (the floating button on a phone).
2. In To, type the recipient's registered Qortal name and pick it from the suggestions; Q-Mail checks every name before sending.
3. Add a subject, write your message and attach files if you like.
4. Press Send (Ctrl or Cmd+Enter on a computer) and approve the publish in Hub.
The mail is encrypted to the recipient and published on QDN under your name.

## Q: Do I need a registered name to use Q-Mail?

A: Yes. Q-Mail sends mail from one Qortal name to another: you need a registered name to send, and the recipient must have one too. Each mail is encrypted with the recipient's public key, which Q-Mail finds through their name; if it says a name has no public key yet, mail to that name can't be encrypted, so it can't be sent.

## Q: Is Q-Mail encrypted?

A: Yes, the contents are. The subject, text and attachments are encrypted to each recipient's public key before they are published on QDN, so only the people it was sent to, and you as the sender, can read them. What isn't hidden is that a mail exists: it is a public QDN record under your name, and its identifier holds the start of the recipient's name.

## Q: How do I download files from Q-Share?

A: Open the share and press Fetch all files, or Download on one file. Your node first fetches the file from peers that hold it; when it's ready, press Save to keep it on your device. Save all as .zip saves every file in one zip (up to 150 MB). The Downloads list shows progress, a Save button on each ready file, and Clear finished.

## Q: Can people see who I send Q-Mail to?

A: Partly. The message itself is encrypted, but each mail is a public QDN record under your name, and its identifier contains the first 20 characters of the recipient's name and the last 6 characters of their address. So anyone browsing QDN can see that you mailed that name, and when. To keep the recipient's name off the record, send to an alias inbox they gave you (Send to alias); the record then shows the alias instead.

## Q: What is an alias in Q-Mail?

A: An alias is an extra inbox label that isn't a registered name. With Send to alias, the mail goes to the alias inbox the recipient told you about instead of their name inbox, so the public QDN record shows the alias rather than their name. It is still encrypted to the recipient and you are still the sender. Cc and Bcc copies aren't sent with an alias. Aliases you watch are listed under Aliases in the mailbox list.

## Q: What do Cc and Bcc do in Q-Mail?

A: Cc: each Cc name gets its own encrypted copy, and everyone who gets the mail can see the Cc names. Bcc: each Bcc name also gets its own encrypted copy and isn't listed in the mail, but every copy is a public QDN record under your name, labelled with that name and sharing one send id with the other copies, so Bcc is not fully hidden. Reply all fills the Cc row for you.

## Q: What are group threads in Q-Mail?

A: Group threads are mail conversations for a Qortal group you belong to, listed under Threads by group. The messages are encrypted to every group member who has a public key, but a thread's title is public. You can start threads and reply to posts, with unread marks. If you don't use them, turn off Settings → Mail → Show group threads, which also makes Q-Mail load faster.

## Q: What is Publish Q-Mail State?

A: Your read and unread marks, archive and some settings are kept on this device. Publish Q-Mail State (in the mailbox list, or Settings → Sync → Publish mail state now) saves them to QDN as one document encrypted to you, which costs one QDN publish. On another device Q-Mail offers to load that state once per sign-in, or loads it by itself if you turn on Always fetch and apply published mail state.

## Q: Can I delete a Q-Mail I sent?

## Q: How does a + app like Q-Share+ become the official version?

A: Each + app goes through four stages: it is built in the Q-Apps+ project, published under its + name on QDN as a pre-release testground, tested with the community with fixes released as 1.0.x versions, and then offered to the original app as a pull request for its maintainers. Until that happens the stable app is the one to use. Q-Share 2.0 and Q-Mail 4.0 went all the way.

## Q: Does opening a Q-Mail mark it as read?

A: Yes. Opening a message in your inbox or an alias inbox marks it read, and a sender's group stops being bold once all its messages are read. Read marks stay after a reload on this device; publish your mail state to carry them to another device.

## Q: Which less common file types does Q-Share handle?

A: Subtitle files (.srt, .vtt) and checksum files (.md5, .sha…) preview as text, audiobooks (.m4b) and WebM audio (.weba) play, and comic books (.cbz, .cbr) are listed as documents to download. Other files can always be downloaded and saved.

## Q: How do I open someone's Q-Tube channel?

## Q: How does Q-Shop show prices in ARRR?

A: If a shop accepts Pirate Chain and you choose ARRR as the coin, Q-Shop shows the rate it uses as 1 QORT = … ARRR, worked out from the latest trades on Qortal's trade system. If it can't get a rate, it goes back to QORT.

## Q: What is an asset's primary group in Q-Assets?

A: When you issue an asset you can pick a Qortal group as its primary group; Q-Assets calls it optional but recommended. The issuer can then send notifications and an encrypted Q-Mail to that group's members, and a private asset must use a private group.

## Q: What does the Q-Node peer list show?

A: For each peer your node is connected to, Q-Node shows its address, the direction of the connection, the handshake status, the peer's last block height, its Core version and how long it has been connected, with actions such as remove and force sync. The QDN data peers have a list of their own.

## Q: Does the Welcome to Qortal app show live network numbers?

## Q: Where can I see recent super likes on Q-Tube?

## Q: Why can't I open a web link in a Q-Share description?

A: Qortal Hub can't open ordinary web, email or phone links from inside a Q-App. In a share's description Q-Share offers to copy such a link instead (Copy web link), so you can paste it into your browser. qortal:// links in descriptions open normally in Hub or GO.
