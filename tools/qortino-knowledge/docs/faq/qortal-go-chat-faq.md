## Q: How do I open the chat in Qortal GO?

A: Tap the round Chat button at the bottom of GO, next to Home and Apps, or swipe sideways between the sections. Chat opens Q-Chat with two lists under one header: Groups and DMs. Tap the switch at the top or swipe the list sideways to move between them. GO keeps Chat ready in the background, so coming back to it is instant.

## Q: What is the difference between Groups and DMs in GO chat?

A: Groups are chats for the members of a Qortal group, split into channels by topic. DMs are private chats between you and one other account. Saved Messages, your private notes, sits at the top of the DMs list. Switch between the two lists with the Groups / DMs switch or a sideways swipe.

## Q: How does chat work in Qortal GO?

A: Q-Chat in GO, like in Hub 3.0, runs over Reticulum, a peer-to-peer network: messages go from device to device instead of through a server or the blockchain. Every message is signed with your Qortal account, and group membership comes from the Qortal chain. Your phone keeps its own chat database and fetches missed messages from other members' devices. Messages are not blockchain transactions, so sending one costs no QORT.

## Q: Do I pay a fee to send a chat message in GO?

A: No. Chat messages in GO travel over Reticulum and are not blockchain transactions, so they cost no QORT. Actions that are recorded on the Qortal chain still have their normal fee, for example joining or creating a group, making someone an admin or registering a name.

## Q: Why isn't my DM getting delivered in GO?

A: A DM travels directly between devices, so it needs your phone and one of the recipient's devices online at the same time. GO keeps the message and retries for up to 7 days. Keeping GO open, or using background sync while charging, gives it more chances. A Hub that runs at home on your account can also deliver it for you.

## Q: What are GO's Background sync modes?

A: Background sync keeps chat working while GO is not on the screen. Off: chat stops about a minute after you leave and catches up when you return. While charging (the default): GO keeps syncing while the phone charges. Always: it keeps syncing whenever GO runs in the background, which uses noticeably more battery. Set it in the notifications panel, under the gear.
