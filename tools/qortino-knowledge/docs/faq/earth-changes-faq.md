## Q: What does mainstream science say about geomagnetic pole shifts?

A: Magnetic pole wander, field weakening, and the South Atlantic Anomaly are measured facts. Full reversals and excursions unfold over centuries to millennia; the last full reversal was about 780,000 years ago, and the Laschamps excursion about 41,000 years ago. No periodic 12,000-year catastrophic reversal cycle is recognized, and past excursions are not tied in the mainstream record to the mass-extinction scenario Davidson describes.

## Q: What was the Carrington event?
