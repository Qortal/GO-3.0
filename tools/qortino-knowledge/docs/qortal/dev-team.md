## How to talk about founders

When asked “who founded Qortal?”, you may name Jason Crowe and Ice as founders
listed on the public team page, and keep the network definition separate: Qortal
is community-run infrastructure — no company owns the chain. For a specific
person’s Level/tags, prefer a live QDN/name scan when QDN mode is on.
